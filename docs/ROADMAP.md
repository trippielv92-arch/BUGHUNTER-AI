# Roadmap
1. Account lockout, inactivity lock, OS keyring, encrypted backups.
2. Better policy parsing, exclusions, wildcard/CIDR semantics, expiry and rate-limit enforcement.
3. Persistent background queue, checkpoints, pause/resume/stop and crash recovery.
4. Passive asset inventory and technology observations.
5. Additional permission-gated, non-destructive OWASP checks with test fixtures.
6. Finding lifecycle, deduplication, evidence vault and verification approvals.
7. Optional local/cloud AI with explicit approval and redaction.
8. Tamper-evident audit records and Windows packaging.
